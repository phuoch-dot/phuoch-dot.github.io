Technical Projects

Autonomous Mobile Stair-Climbing Robot

Arduino Mega Systems Integration Robotics

Engineered a 10 kg robotics system designed to navigate a 20-meter course with a 33-degree incline while fitting within a strict 0.5m x 0.5m x 0.5m dimension constraint. Features a custom egg recovery mechanism actuated by dual-shaft E&S 12V 27RPM worm gear motors.

Hardware Architecture: Driven by 5202 Series Yellow Jacket planetary gear motors and a #35 chain drive. Power distribution managed via an MDD20A motor driver and a 12V 3000mAh NiMH battery with Wagos voltage conversion. Wireless operations controlled over Bluetooth Low Energy using the Dabble app.

Mechanical Hardware Design & CAD

Onshape 3D Printing CAD Modeling

Modeled custom mechanical hardware in Onshape optimized for 3D printing and precise tolerances. Designed a 14 mm x 20 mm closed lug nut featuring a 22 mm socket size and a 60-degree conical seat, engineered to thread by hand perfectly onto an existing 50 mm bolt with calculated thread clearances.

Generated full bills of materials for custom robot chassis components including 3D-printed sprockets, hex shafts, steel rods, bearings, and sonic hubs.
